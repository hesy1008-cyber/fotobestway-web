    Description

    Designed for professional film, television, and studio environments, this versatile boom arm provides a reliable solution for overhead lighting, microphone placement, and mounting a wide range of production accessories. Its robust construction ensures stability while allowing precise positioning, making it an essential tool for controlled studio setups and dynamic on-set workflows.

    The boom arm features an adjustable pivot clamp with an ergonomic ratcheting handle, allowing quick, secure angle adjustments without compromising stability. This system ensures effortless positioning while keeping mounted equipment firmly in place, even during extended shoots.

    For enhanced balance and safety, a durable steel ring is integrated at the end of the arm, making it easy to attach counterweights or sandbags. This added support helps maintain stability when working with heavier lighting fixtures or audio gear.

    With a maximum extension of 76 inches (193 cm) and a compact minimum length of 47 3/8 inches (120.3 cm), the boom arm offers excellent reach while remaining manageable in tighter setups. Its adaptable top head includes a removable spigot that can be installed in three different positions, providing maximum mounting flexibility. The spigot is equipped with both 1/4"-20 and 3/8"-16 threads to accommodate a wide range of industry-standard lighting and accessory mounts.

    Built for durability, flexibility, and professional performance, this boom arm is an ideal addition to any lighting or audio support setup requiring dependable overhead mounting solutions.
